Insurance and billing

Clear answers about costs and insurance.

Our insurance desk helps you understand your cover, arrange cashless treatment where your policy allows it and prepare the paperwork for a claim.

  • {{icon:i-shield}}

    Cashless treatment

    If your insurer or TPA has an arrangement with the hospital, we request pre-authorisation for you and bill the insurer directly, subject to your policy terms.

  • {{icon:i-file}}

    Reimbursement

    If cashless treatment is not available, you pay the hospital and claim the amount from your insurer afterwards. We give you the documents you need.

  • {{icon:i-chat}}

    Estimates

    Ask for a written estimate before a planned procedure or admission, so you know what to expect and what your policy may cover.

Step by step

How cashless treatment works.

  1. Check your cover

    Before a planned admission, share your policy or TPA card with our insurance desk. We will tell you whether cashless treatment is possible for your policy.

  2. Pre-authorisation

    We send the pre-authorisation request with your doctor's notes. For planned treatment, please contact us a few days in advance. For an emergency admission, we start this as soon as possible.

  3. Approval

    Your insurer or TPA reviews the request and confirms the approved amount. We keep you informed while you wait.

  4. Discharge

    At discharge, we send the final bill to the insurer. You pay only for anything not covered, such as non-medical items or amounts above your limit.

Paperwork

Documents to keep ready.

{{icon:i-shield}} For a cashless admission

  • Health insurance card or TPA card
  • Policy document or policy number
  • Photo ID of the patient and the policyholder
  • Your doctor's advice for admission
  • Previous reports related to the condition

{{icon:i-file}} For a reimbursement claim

  • Discharge summary
  • Final bill with itemised breakdown and payment receipts
  • Test reports and prescriptions
  • Pharmacy bills
  • Your insurer's completed claim form
