Preventive health checks

Health checks that help you stay ahead.

Regular check-ups can find problems early, when they are easier to treat. Choose a package that suits your age and health, and our team will help you prepare.

  • {{icon:i-package}}

    Essential Health Check

    A yearly baseline for adults with no major health concerns.

    Typically includes

    • Complete blood count
    • Fasting blood sugar
    • Lipid profile
    • Kidney and liver function tests
    • Urine routine examination
    • ECG
    • Consultation with a physician

    • Vitamin D and vitamin B12
    • Chest X-ray

Before your health check

How to prepare.

{{icon:i-clock}} The day before

  • Fast for 10 to 12 hours before blood tests. Water is fine.
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours
  • Keep taking your regular medicines unless your doctor says otherwise
  • Tell us if you are diabetic, so we can plan your fasting tests safely

{{icon:i-file}} On the day

  • Arrive early in the morning, as most tests are done before breakfast
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing
  • Bring previous reports and a list of your medicines
  • Allow a few hours for all tests and the consultation
