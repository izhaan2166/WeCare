Before you come

Preparing for your visit

A little preparation helps you get the most from your consultation. Please arrive about 15 minutes before your appointment time to allow for registration.

{{icon:i-file}} What to bring

  • A government-issued photo ID
  • Previous prescriptions, discharge summaries and test reports
  • A list of the medicines you currently take
  • Your referral letter, if you have one
  • Insurance card or policy details, if applicable

{{icon:i-chat}} Before your consultation

  • Write down your symptoms and when they started
  • Note the questions you want to ask
  • Check whether you need to fast before any tests
  • Bring a family member or carer if it would help
  • Tell us in advance about any access needs

Visiting someone

Patient and visitor guidelines

Visitors are an important part of recovery. These simple guidelines help us keep every patient safe, comfortable and rested.

Visiting hours
General visiting hours{{hours.visiting}}
Visitors per patientTwo visitors at a time, unless the ward team advises otherwise

{{icon:i-check}} Please do

  • Clean your hands when entering and leaving patient areas
  • Keep phones on silent and voices low
  • Follow any instructions from nursing staff
  • Keep children supervised at all times

{{icon:i-alert}} Please avoid

  • Visiting if you have a fever, cough or infection
  • Bringing outside food without checking with the ward team
  • Taking photographs of other patients or staff
  • Smoking anywhere on the hospital premises
{{icon:i-info}}

Visiting arrangements can change to protect patients, for example during seasonal infections. Please check with the ward team or call reception before you travel.

Costs

Billing and insurance

We aim to be clear about costs before treatment begins. Ask our billing desk for an estimate for consultations, tests or procedures, and for help understanding your bill.

{{icon:i-shield}} Health insurance

If you plan to use health insurance, bring your policy details and photo ID. Our team can tell you which insurers and arrangements the hospital works with and what paperwork you will need.

{{icon:i-file}} Estimates and receipts

Ask for a written estimate before a planned procedure. Itemised bills and receipts are available on request at the billing desk.

Respect and privacy

Your rights as a patient

Every patient has the right to respectful care, clear information, privacy, and to take part in decisions about their treatment. You can ask questions, request copies of your records and give feedback at any time.

  • To be treated with dignity, without discrimination
  • To receive information about your condition and treatment options in a language you understand
  • To give or withhold consent before any procedure
  • To have your personal and medical information kept confidential
  • To raise a concern or complaint and receive a response
