About {{name}}

Specialist care that still feels personal.

When your care involves more than one specialist, your doctors coordinate with each other so you are not left to connect the dots. From your first appointment to follow-up, you will have clear information about what is happening and why.

Why patients choose us

Care that puts you at the centre of every decision.

Being unwell is stressful enough. We focus on the things that make a hospital visit easier: being heard, being informed and knowing who to ask.

{{img:why:half}}

  1. Patient-first approach

    Your concerns, preferences and questions shape your plan of care. We explain options and respect your choices.

  2. Experienced medical professionals

    Consultants and nursing teams who work within their specialities and keep learning as medicine moves forward.

  3. Coordinated patient care

    When more than one department is involved, your doctors plan together so your care feels like one journey.

  4. Modern healthcare environment

    Clean, calm and accessible spaces designed with patient comfort, privacy and dignity in mind.

  5. Clear communication

    Plain-language explanations, written summaries where helpful, and time for your family's questions.

  6. Convenient appointment enquiries

    Request an appointment online or by phone, and our team will contact you to confirm a suitable time.

Your visit, step by step

What to expect when you come to see us.

  1. Request an appointment

    Send an enquiry online or call the appointments desk with your preferred department and time.

  2. Receive confirmation

    Our team contacts you to confirm the doctor, date and time, and anything to bring.

  3. Arrive and register

    Come 15 minutes early. Reception will help you register and guide you to the department.

  4. Consultation and next steps

    Your doctor explains findings and options, and you leave knowing what happens next.

Contact us {{icon:i-arrow:icon icon--sm}}

You can request an appointment using our online appointment form or by calling the appointments desk on {{appointmentsPhone.display}}. Our team will contact you to confirm the date, time and consultant. An appointment is confirmed only once you hear back from us.

General visiting hours are {{hours.visiting}}. Some areas may have different arrangements to protect patients who need extra rest. Please check with the ward team or read our visitor guidelines before you come.

Browse our specialities to see the kinds of concerns each department looks after. If you are still unsure, choose "Not sure" on the appointment form or call us, and our team will help direct you. General Medicine is often a good starting point for adults.

Bring a photo ID, any previous prescriptions, test reports and a list of medicines you take. It helps to write down your symptoms and questions beforehand. See preparing for your visit for a full checklist.

Call our main line on {{phone.display}}, email {{email}} or use the contact form. For a medical emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.
