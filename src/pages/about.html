Our name

The meaning behind our name

Our name is our promise. WeCare means care is shared: every specialist, nurse and team member working together on one clear plan for you.

Many patients see more than one doctor during their treatment. Too often, that means repeating their history, carrying reports between rooms and piecing together advice on their own. We set out to make that experience simpler.

Our departments share information with your consent, consultants discuss your care with each other when it helps, and you always know who to contact with a question. It is a quieter, more considered kind of hospital experience, and we think it leads to better conversations and better decisions.

What guides us

Four principles behind every consultation.

These principles shape how our teams work with patients and with each other, from the reception desk to the consultation room.

  1. Principle 1

    Listen first

    We begin by understanding your concerns, your history and what matters to you, before we suggest any test or treatment.

  2. Principle 2

    Explain clearly

    Diagnoses, options and costs are explained in plain language, and you are always welcome to ask a question twice.

  3. Principle 3

    Work as one team

    Specialists, nurses and support staff coordinate around you, so your care feels joined up rather than handed over.

  4. Principle 4

    Respect every person

    Every patient is treated with dignity, privacy and kindness, whatever their age, background or reason for visiting.

How your care is organised

One plan, shared by everyone looking after you.

Coordination is designed into how a visit works, so you spend less time explaining and more time understanding.

  • {{icon:i-person}}

    A consultant who knows your case

    Your treating consultant oversees your plan and is your main medical point of reference throughout.

  • {{icon:i-route}}

    Departments that talk to each other

    When another speciality is needed, referrals happen within the hospital and your records travel with you.

  • {{icon:i-file}}

    Clear summaries and follow-up

    You leave with written next steps, and follow-up appointments are planned before you go home.

Our commitments to you

What every patient can expect from us.

Our patient commitments set out the standard of respect and communication you should receive at every visit.

  • {{icon:i-hand}}

    Dignity and respect

    Courteous care that respects your values, culture and personal choices.

  • {{icon:i-shield}}

    Privacy and confidentiality

    Your health information is handled carefully and shared only with your consent or where the law requires.

  • {{icon:i-chat}}

    Information you understand

    Clear explanations of your condition, treatment options and estimated costs.

  • {{icon:i-check}}

    Informed consent

    No procedure goes ahead until you have understood it and agreed to it.

  • {{icon:i-team}}

    Family involvement

    The people you choose can be included in conversations about your care.

  • {{icon:i-leaf}}

    A voice in improving care

    Your feedback is welcomed and reviewed, so we can keep making visits better.
