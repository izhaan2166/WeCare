Privacy Policy

How we handle personal information shared through this website.

Draft for review. This policy is a starting template for the website concept. It must be reviewed by the hospital's legal and compliance advisers, including against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, before the website goes live.

Who we are

This website is operated by {{name}}. You can contact us about privacy at {{email}}.

Information we collect

When you use an enquiry form on this website, we ask for the details needed to respond, such as your name, mobile number, email address, preferred department and appointment time. We ask you not to share detailed medical information through website forms.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your appointment request or enquiry
  • To contact you about the appointment you asked for
  • To improve how the website and our services work

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with staff and service providers who need it to respond to you, or where the law requires.

How long we keep it

Enquiry information is kept only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, and then securely deleted, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to be contacted, by writing to {{email}}.

How form enquiries are sent

When you complete a form, the details are checked in your browser and then sent in one of two ways. If you choose WhatsApp or email, your own app opens with the message filled in and nothing is sent until you press send. Where the hospital has connected a secure form service, your enquiry is sent directly to the hospital team over an encrypted connection. Please do not include detailed medical history in a website form.
