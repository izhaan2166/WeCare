Choosing a consultant

Help with choosing the right doctor.

You can request a specific consultant, or let our appointments team match you with the right specialist for your concern.

  1. Start with your concern

    Choose the department that fits best. If you are unsure, General Medicine or our appointments team can guide you.

  2. Request a preferred doctor

    Name a consultant on the appointment form if you have one in mind, or tell us if you would like the earliest available appointment.

  3. Ask about second opinions

    If you would like a second opinion on a diagnosis or treatment plan, mention it when you request your appointment.
