Seeing a specialist

How a specialist appointment works.

  1. Choose a department

    Pick the speciality that matches your concern, or ask us to guide you. A referral letter is helpful but not always required.

  2. Confirm your appointment

    Our team calls you to agree a date, time and consultant, and lets you know if any tests are needed beforehand.

  3. Meet your consultant

    Your consultant reviews your history, examines you and talks through findings and next steps with you.
